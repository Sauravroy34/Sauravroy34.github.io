I’m **Saurav Kumar Roy**. I use this site to document hands-on work where machine learning meets chemistry, scientific computing, and open-source software.

[Start with OLMO learns chemistry →](/posts/olmo_learns_chemistry/)

## Chemistry and machine learning

### OLMO learns chemistry

I continued pre-training OLMo-7B on ZINC20 molecules and USPTO reactions, then compared the result with chemistry-specific transformer models and a random forest baseline.

[Read the OLMO project notes](/posts/olmo_learns_chemistry/)

### MolGAN

I implemented MolGAN from scratch in PyTorch, combining molecular graph generation with a reinforcement-learning objective for properties such as solubility and synthesizability.

[See the MolGAN implementation](/posts/molgan/)
